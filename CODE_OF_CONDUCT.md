# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participation must be professional, technically honest, and welcoming. Be
respectful across differences in experience, identity, language, and
background. Critique code and evidence, not people.

Acceptable participation includes reproducible feedback, clear uncertainty,
open correction, and patient technical explanation. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and
publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Technical Integrity

Do not fabricate tests, conceal regressions, misstate subsystem status, or
present CaribeOS as an Apple product. Constructive disagreement about boot
architecture, ABI behavior, security, evidence, or licensing is welcome.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close interactions, or restrict
participation when behavior violates this standard. Contact the repository
owner privately through the contact methods on their GitHub profile for a
sensitive conduct report; do not post private details in a public issue.

Reports will be considered in context and handled with as much confidentiality
as the platform permits. Retaliation against good-faith reporters is prohibited.
